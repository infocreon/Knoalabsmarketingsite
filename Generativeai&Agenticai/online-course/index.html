Do I need coding or an IT background?

No. Part 1 teaches Python from scratch: setup, syntax, functions and data structures, then OOP, APIs and advanced Python for AI. Basic computer skills and a laptop with 8 GB RAM are enough. If you already code, you can move faster through Part 1.

Which certificate do I get?

You get a Knoa Labs certificate on successful completion. You also finish with hands-on labs and a real capstone project reviewed by an instructor panel, which you can show employers.

What is the course fee? Is EMI available?

We send the full fee structure, EMI options and batch schedule on WhatsApp, usually within 30 minutes (9am–8pm). Just share your details in the form above.

How long is the course and what are the class timings?

The course runs for 3–4 months, with live online sessions of up to 3 hours each. Our advisor shares the exact batch timings so you can pick one that fits around college or work.

What is Agentic AI and why does it matter?

Agentic AI systems plan, use tools and take action with far less human help than a normal chatbot, for example an agent that researches online, runs code and writes a report. Part 3 (Modules 12–19) is dedicated to building them with LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen and MCP.

Is there a placement guarantee?

No, and be careful with anyone who promises one. We give placement support: resume and LinkedIn guidance, interview preparation, a capstone project you can demo, and internship connections.
