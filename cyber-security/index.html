Curriculum · 12 modules

From zero to your first pentest report

From Linux and networking basics to web, API, mobile and AI security. You learn a concept, then you hack it in your own lab.

  • 12modules
  • 3–4months

Download Full Syllabus (PDF) Book Free Demo Class
01Cybersecurity Foundations & Security MindsetCIA triad · Kill chain · Red, blue & purple teams

CIA triad, defence-in-depth and zero trust, ethical vs malicious hacking, the cyber kill chain, CVEs and vulnerability scoring, and how VA, pentesting and red teaming differ.

02Linux Fundamentals for Security ProfessionalsKali Linux · VirtualBox · Bash

Set up Kali Linux with VirtualBox snapshots, then learn the file system, core commands, permissions (chmod, chown, SUID/SGID), Bash scripting and Linux logs for security monitoring.

03Networking Fundamentals & Protocol AnalysisWireshark

Network types and topologies, OSI vs TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS, DHCP, ARP and SSH, TCP/UDP handshakes and attack surfaces, IP addressing and subnetting, and packet analysis with Wireshark.

04Windows Fundamentals & Cloud Security BasicsWindows · NTFS · Cloud IAM

Windows architecture, NTFS permissions and the registry, then cloud models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), the shared responsibility model and IAM fundamentals.

05Network & Wireless ExploitationNmap · Metasploit · Hydra · John the Ripper · Aircrack-ng

OSINT and reconnaissance, active scanning with Nmap, packet crafting with Hping3 and Netcat, password cracking, buffer overflow basics, the Metasploit Framework and wireless security.

06Web Application Fundamentals & Secure DevelopmentApache2 · SQL · OWASP Top 10

How web apps work end to end: hosting on Linux with Apache2, web programming and SQL for security testing, an introduction to the OWASP Top 10 and secure coding principles.

07Web & Network Application Security: Hands-On LabsBurp Suite · OWASP ZAP · 22 hrs of labs

SQL injection, XSS, broken access control and IDOR, CSRF vs SSRF, authentication and session flaws, CORS misconfigurations, file upload to RCE, and insecure deserialization.

08API Penetration TestingOWASP API Top 10 · JWT

API fundamentals, the OWASP API Security Top 10, JWT attacks, and API enumeration and fuzzing.

09Mobile Application Penetration TestingAPK decompilation · Burp Suite

Mobile app architecture, the OWASP Mobile Top 10, static analysis and APK decompilation, and dynamic analysis with Burp Suite.

10AI / LLM SecurityOWASP Top 10 for LLMs · Prompt injection

The OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications, direct and indirect prompt injection, sensitive data leakage in AI systems, securing AI-integrated apps and AI red-teaming.

11Penetration Testing Reporting & DocumentationProfessional pentest report

Report structure and risk ratings, writing for technical and business readers, evidence handling and chain of custody, and industry report standards.

12Career Readiness: Resume, Certifications & Interview PrepResume · Portfolio · Mock interviews

A cybersecurity resume and portfolio, your certification roadmap, and technical and behavioural interview practice.

How do the live online classes work? What if my internet drops?

You join live classes from your laptop. The trainer teaches and hacks live on screen while you follow along in your own lab, and you can ask questions by voice or chat at any time. If your internet drops, just rejoin. Every class is recorded and added to your dashboard, so you never miss a topic.

Why join a live course instead of Udemy or YouTube?

Free and recorded videos are useful for learning topics, but nobody checks your work, answers your doubts the same day or keeps you on schedule. In a live batch, a working security engineer teaches you in real time, reviews your labs, and helps you build a capstone pentest report you can show employers.

What is the course fee?

We send the full fee structure and any scholarship you qualify for on WhatsApp, usually within 30 minutes (9am–8pm). Just share your name and number in the form above.

Do I need an IT or coding background?

No. The course starts from zero, with computer basics, networking and Linux first. If you can use a computer and can give a few hours each week, you can do this. Coding helps later but isn't needed to start.

What laptop and internet do I need?

A laptop with at least 8 GB RAM (Windows, Mac or Linux), a stable internet connection and headphones. We help you set up your lab in the first week.

What are the class timings? Can I manage with college or a job?

Yes. Live classes are on weekends only: Saturday and Sunday, 2–3 hours a day, with a morning or an afternoon batch. Your weekdays stay free for college or work, and you can practise the labs whenever it suits you.

What if I miss a class?

Watch the recording in your dashboard (you keep lifetime access) and post any doubts in the batch WhatsApp group. The trainer or a mentor answers the same day.

Is there a placement guarantee?

No, and be careful with anyone who promises one. We give placement support: a resume and LinkedIn makeover, mock interviews, a capstone portfolio and honest advice on which roles to apply for. Getting the job depends on your skills and effort, and we help you build both.

Which certificate do I get? Does it prepare me for CEH / Security+?

You get a Knoa Labs course completion certificate after you finish the labs and the capstone project. The syllabus covers most topics in CEH and CompTIA Security+ and we give you a certification roadmap, but the official exams and their fees are separate.

How long is the course?

3–4 months of live weekend classes (Saturday and Sunday, 2–3 hours a day). Recordings stay available for life, so you can revise after the course ends.

Is learning ethical hacking legal?

Yes. Ethical hacking is a recognised profession in India and around the world. You only practise on lab machines and targets you have permission to test. Hacking any system without written permission is a crime under India's IT Act, 2000, and we teach you where that line is from day one.
