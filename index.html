Common questions about Knoa Labs courses

What courses does Knoa Labs offer online?

Knoa Labs offers two live online courses: Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking (12 modules, 3–4 months) and Generative AI & Agentic AI (19 modules, 3–4 months). Both include hands-on labs, a capstone project and placement support.

Are the classes live or recorded?

Both courses are taught live online. Cyber Security classes run on Saturdays and Sundays, 2–3 hours a day, and recordings stay available for life. Gen AI & Agentic AI sessions run up to 3 hours each, and an advisor shares the exact batch timings.

Do I need an IT or coding background?

No. The Cyber Security course starts with computer basics, networking and Linux, and the Gen AI & Agentic AI course teaches Python from scratch. You need a laptop with 8 GB RAM, a stable internet connection and a few hours each week.

Is there a placement guarantee?

No. Knoa Labs gives placement support, including resume and LinkedIn guidance, interview preparation and a capstone project you can show employers, but it does not guarantee a job.

How do I get the fees and next batch details?

Fill in the enquiry form on either course page or message Knoa Labs on WhatsApp at +91 98957 05599. An advisor replies with the fee structure and batch timings, usually within 30 minutes (9am–8pm).

Where is Knoa Labs based?

Knoa Labs Private Limited is based in Calicut (Kozhikode), Kerala, India. The courses are taught live online.
